# ClubOS v39 — Member Portal UI/UX Redesign

Presentation/navigation redesign only. Existing Member Portal business logic and old URLs are preserved.

- Five main areas: Home, Events, Club, Shop, Me.
- Desktop uses a dark left sidebar; mobile uses fixed bottom navigation.
- Existing direct routes remain available and map visually to the appropriate main section.
- Home prioritises Action Required, Upcoming events, Latest from the Club, and small Quick Access links.
- Notification bell shows pending voting and unread club updates.
- Unread updates include announcements, awards, new documents and newly published events.
- Club groups voting, news, committee, documents and awards.
- Shop groups merchandise, orders and donations while reusing the existing purchase/donation components.
- Me groups profile, membership, payments, tickets and awards, and shows a compact digital member card.
- Events use internal tabs: Upcoming, Calendar, My Tickets and Past Events.
- Voting uses internal tabs: Active Motions, Past Motions and Voting History; vote logic is unchanged.
- Payment history has a mobile-friendly row layout while retaining the desktop table.
- No database migration is required.
