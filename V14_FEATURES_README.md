# ClubOS v14 — Awards & Recognition

## Added
- Organisation-defined award types (e.g. Volunteer of the Year, Best Bowler, Club Spirit Award).
- Searchable member picker by name, member number or email.
- Give/edit/remove awards with award date, year, season, citation, internal notes and visibility.
- Public/member-visible awards automatically appear in Member Portal > News & Updates.
- Private awards remain visible only to the recipient and authorised governance admins.
- Awards appear as a permanent history on the recipient's Member Portal profile.
- Awards appear as a dedicated tab on the admin member record.
- Demo award types and sample member awards are included.
