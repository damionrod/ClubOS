# ClubOS v37

Targeted changes only:
- Sports label under Teams renamed to Group Type.
- Communications Add Record publishes articles to Member News & Updates, with Member Type audience, date and optional file/image attachment.
- Committee roles can be created/edited; existing members can be searched and appointed. Appointments publish to News & Updates.
- Organisations & Contacts supports multiple editable contact people per organisation.
- Documents support PDF/Word/Excel attachments and member visibility through Member Portal > More > Club Documents.
- SQL migration required: supabase/UPGRADE_COMPACT_V37.sql
